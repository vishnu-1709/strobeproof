How to read this

  • A transition is a change in relative luminance of at least 0.10 where the darker side is below 0.80. Two opposing transitions make a flash.
  • FAIL means 7 or more transitions (more than 3 flashes) in some 1-second window, over more than 25% of a 10° field of view (21,824 px² at 1024×768).
  • Case 15 runs case 2 through Calm Mode (grayscale(1) brightness(0.32)). It passes because Calm Mode caps white at relative luminance 0.084, below the 0.10 needed for any transition.
Source: W3C, Understanding SC 2.3.1. Strobeproof reduces exposure; it is not a medical device.

Strobeproof is on

It checks GIFs and videos for fast flashing before you see them at full brightness. Anything it hasn't checked yet is shown in Calm Mode: dimmed and grey, so it can't flash brightly.

One shortcut to remember: Alt + Shift + F calms everything on the page. Press it again to restore.

On any calmed item, you can choose Keep Calm, Show original, or Always allow this site. Esc always takes you back to Calm.

What it can't check
  • Games and drawings made with canvas, page animations, and animated backgrounds.
  • Some videos can't be read (for example protected films or videos from other sites). These stay in Calm Mode unless you choose to play them normally.
  • Normal videos are watched as they play. If flashing starts, it switches to Calm Mode within a fraction of a second, not instantly.
  • Very bright HDR screens can show brighter than Calm Mode expects.
  • Everyone is different. The limits follow the web's flash-safety guideline for a typical screen and distance.
  • It reduces exposure. It is not a medical device and can't catch everything.

Works in Chrome, Edge and Brave. Strobeproof has no server and no account: everything is checked on this computer.
